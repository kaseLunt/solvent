C · Editorial

A live risk report, not a dashboard. Light, warm paper; serif headlines that state the finding; charts with annotations and captions that carry the story; numbers in tabular sans. Nothing else in crypto looks like this, which is the point — and the honest-rendering doctrine reads naturally as journalism rather than as disclaimers.

Register: FT visual journalism · NYT Upshot · The Economist · Stripe's annual letters.

Not shown: the light theme for A and B (it exists today and stays), and the dark theme for C (would be a warm charcoal, not the current teal-black). Whichever you pick, the next screen is that direction applied to the Inspector and Scenarios pages so you can see it hold across the app.
