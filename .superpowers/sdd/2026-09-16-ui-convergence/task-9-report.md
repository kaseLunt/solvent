## Deviations (with reasons)
1. **`readsAsAnswer` is wider than the ruling's example.** The ruling's example says a body reads only when its Cash row is present and clean. The landed law (`bookOf`'s comment and the existing pin "an honest answer releases the hold: a withheld book…") says withheld / not-modelled ARE answers that release the hold. With the literal predicate, result → withheld 200 → failure would resurrect the old result under "Run again failed", contradicting that pin's law. So: envelope clean AND (Cash refused, or no Cash row, or the Cash row classifies clean with a non-contradictory matrix). Definition-free, as ruled. One residual edge: a definition that does not model Cash + a stray malformed Cash row → the view says not-modelled while the record does not hold that body; harmless (an older hold stands).
2. **`withRunning` takes the predicate too.** The hold moves at the ASK (that is where the bug lived); `withSettled` alone cannot fix it without storing a verdict on the record, which would touch every `RunRecord` literal in the specs.
3. **Set faults are worded by the classifier** ("… is outside the wire contract"), replacing the close-out's "is not a list" strings, and per-result list faults remain a per-ROW refusal rather than failing the whole Compare.
4. **`LabSurface.tsx` and `lab-library.spec.ts` untouched** — nothing in them needed to change.
