# Design coherence — the director's plan (verbatim, rendered)

Source: workflow wf_f5b9986e-d25 (four audit lenses — visual system, information, code consistency, responsive; the hiring-evaluator lens failed on its agent type's pinned model — a design-director synthesis that tried to refute every finding against the captures, and a completeness critic). The integrator's amendments in `design-amendments.md` SUPERSEDE this document where they conflict.

## Verdict

The honesty machinery and the Console kit underneath Solvent are good, but what a reader sees is three design generations stitched together. There are four page frames, two chart families, a legacy drawer and a legacy type scale, six ways of formatting money, three time formats, and a green that means health, freshness, connection and 'serving' in the same viewport. It is not at the Linear/Stripe/FT bar today. At 1024px and 390px it is visibly broken: the header overflows, tables cut off their verdict column, and the Activity table is unreadable. Almost all of this comes from a few system gaps: no frame owned by the shell, no single tone grammar, no single money/time/percent vocabulary in web/lib, and a handful of missing kit parts (toggle group, legacy fold, step strip, state card, table scroll region). So the six parallel areas below, system areas first, can make every page one instrument without changing a backend, a calculation or an honest state.
