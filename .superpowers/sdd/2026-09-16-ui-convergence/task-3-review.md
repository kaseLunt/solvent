## The two gate items (recommendations)

**Legend moved into the drawer.** What still carries the metric without a key: the pressed metric button directly above the plot (`METRIC_LABELS`, `HistoryChart.tsx:84-93`), the SVG's accessible name (`chartLabel`), the y-max ledger string ("$…" for money, a bare integer for counts), the newest-value label at the point, and the card's finding line. So *which metric* is legible. What is not legible without hover, click or the drawer is the meaning of the two gap glyphs — the dashed tick (absent) and the tick with the small orange outlined square (withheld). The Buckets chip gives the counts and the headline states the tally in words, but nothing maps count → glyph; each tick carries a `<title>` naming its reason and a click opens the record, so the meaning is one hover or one click away. Recommendation for the gate: add a compact two-item mark key inside the `ChartCard` beneath the plot (`--type-small`; e.g. "┆ absent · ▢ withheld", words from the lib, a `history-chart-key` pin), and keep the six method notes in the drawer. The glyph→meaning map is the one thing no other element on the page supplies, and it is two words; the full doctrine stays where R3 put it.

**Lowercase-leading H1.** `observatoryTakeaway` returns "debt $… across N account(s) as of bucket …; …" / "newest bucket … withheld (…) — no numbers served for it…" / "no bucket in this window is backed by a wire row…" (`observatory-series.ts:433-449`), and `VerdictHeader` prints it as the `<h1>`. The sibling pages' computed headlines begin with a digit ("50 chain action(s) loaded…", "8 read-only operations…"), so History is the only page whose H1 begins with a lowercase word. Recommendation: capitalise the first character **inside `observatoryTakeaway` itself** (one change in the module, with its own unit pins in `observatory-series.spec.ts` following) rather than at the view — the history pins compute from the function and follow for free, and R2's "headline equals the module's sentence" stays literally true. A view-level `capitalise()` would break the `toEqual(observatoryTakeaway(...))` pin the brief asked for. The "bucket(s)" / "account(s)" hedges are the same register question and can ride the same change if the gate wants them; neither is a blocker.
