# Design coherence — the four audit lenses (verbatim findings)

## Lens: visual-system

**Verdict.** This is not yet at a Linear/Stripe/FT bar. The kit and the palette are sound, and the honest states are real. But the product reads as three design generations stitched together: four different page frames, three type scales still live, two chart families, a pill or toggle for every page, and time, money and percent each typeset several ways. Most of it traces to a handful of kit and lib gaps (no shared page frame, legacy tokens never retired, no single formatter for instants and account money, tone mapped per page). About eight system-level fixes would make every page recognisably the same instrument. The one doctrine-3 breach (green and crit on records and posture) needs to be fixed before this is shown to anyone.

## Lens: information
